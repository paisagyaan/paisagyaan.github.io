Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

In short: this website has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics, and the calculators run entirely in your browser. Our publishing tool is private: only the channel owner uses it, to manage our own YouTube channels.

1. Who we are

Paisa Gyaan and Billion Dollar Lies are YouTube channels run by an individual creator based in India ("we", "us"). This policy covers two things: this website, and the private tool we use to manage our channels.

Contact: sanamsurveer@gmail.com

2. This website

  • No accounts or forms. You cannot sign in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No cookies, analytics or advertising. The site sets no cookies, uses no local storage, and loads no tracking, analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Calculators. The numbers you type into a calculator are processed only in your browser. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are gone when you close or reload the page.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, in its server logs. We do not receive those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Links. Links to YouTube and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

3. Our YouTube publishing tool

We manage our channels with a private tool named sanamsurveer_channels ("the tool"). It runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and the channel owner is its only user.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Its use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the tool can access the following for our own two channels:

  • channel details and settings (name, description, keywords, branding);
  • our videos and their details (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, privacy status and schedule), including uploading our own videos;
  • our playlists;
  • comments on our own videos;
  • channel membership information, where it exists;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for our channels, including revenue reports.

The tool also reads public YouTube data that anyone can see, such as the titles and view counts of public videos, to research topics.

5. How we use it

  • to publish and update our own videos, playlists and channel pages;
  • to understand how our own videos perform, so we can improve them;
  • to read questions viewers leave in public comments on our videos, so we can answer them and plan future videos;
  • to see which topics are of public interest.

We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or combine it with data from other sources to identify or profile individual people.

6. What is stored, and where

  • Sign-in tokens. The access tokens Google issues are stored in a file on the channel owner's computer. They are not uploaded to any server.
  • Our own channel data. Channel identifiers, video details and analytics reports for our own channels may be kept on that computer for as long as the tool has access.
  • Public data and comments. Public YouTube data about other channels' videos, and the text of public comments, is kept for no more than 30 days and then deleted or refreshed.
  • Cookies and devices. The tool places no cookies and collects nothing from any viewer's device.

7. Sharing

Data obtained through YouTube API Services is not shared with third parties, except that parts of it may be processed by service providers we use to produce our videos and plan our work (for example an AI assistant used to summarise our own analytics). They process it only on our instructions.

8. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Stored tokens and data are deleted by removing the tool's files from the channel owner's computer. After access is revoked, stored data obtained through that access is deleted within 30 days.
  • If you believe the tool holds information about you, such as a public comment you posted, and you want it deleted, write to us at the address above and we will delete it.

9. Children

This website and our channels are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
